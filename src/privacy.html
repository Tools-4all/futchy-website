Privacy Policy

How the FUTCHY iPhone game and the website futchy.co treat your information.

Last updated: {{updated}}

In short. You do not need an account to play FUTCHY. Your game progress and settings are stored mainly on your device. The game shows optional rewarded ads through third-party advertising services, and where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent first. FUTCHY does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. This website sets no cookies and has no analytics.

1. Who we are

FUTCHY is a general-audience mobile beach footvolley game for iOS. This policy covers the FUTCHY app and the website at futchy.co. For any privacy question, write to {{email}}.

2. Information on your device

  • No account. FUTCHY does not require you to create an account, sign in, or give us your name or email address in order to play.
  • Local progress and settings. Your game progress, choices and settings are stored primarily on your device. Deleting the app removes this local data from your device. Copies held in your own device or iCloud backups are controlled by your device settings.

3. Rewarded advertising

FUTCHY offers rewarded ads: you can choose to watch a short ad in exchange for an in-game reward. To show these ads the game uses the following third-party services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), an ad mediation service that requests ads from ad networks. See the LevelPlay GDPR compliance page and the LevelPlay US state privacy laws page.
  • Unity Ads, an ad network. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy FAQ and the Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), used only to manage consent where required. See Google's Privacy Policy and the UMP documentation.

When an ad is requested or shown, these providers may process technical and advertising-related information from your device, for example identifiers associated with your device, your approximate location derived from your connection, information about how you interact with ads, and technical diagnostics. They use it to deliver and measure ads, to limit how often you see an ad, and to prevent fraud. The exact information depends on the provider, your location and your consent choices, and is described in the providers' own notices linked above. We do not control how those providers handle information under their own policies.

4. Consent and your choices

  • Consent first. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game uses Google UMP to show a consent message. The game resolves your privacy state before the advertising services are started.
  • If you decline. Declining consent may result in non-personalized advertising, where that is legally and technically permitted.
  • If your privacy state cannot be determined. If the game cannot resolve your privacy state, rewarded ads are unavailable for that session. The rest of the game is not affected.
  • Ads are your choice. Rewarded ads appear only when you choose to watch one.

5. Tracking

FUTCHY does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not declare tracking under Apple's definition: the game does not use your data to follow you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising.

6. Children

FUTCHY is a general-audience game. It is not a Kids Category app and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has given us information, or you have concerns about an ad shown in the game, please contact us so we can look into it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal information, such as the rights to access, correct or delete it, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because FUTCHY has no account system, we generally hold no personal profile about you. Information processed by the advertising providers is governed by their policies, and you can use the links in section 3 to exercise your rights with them. If you are not sure where to turn, write to {{email}} and we will help point you to the right place.

8. This website

futchy.co is a static website. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising tools, and does not load third-party scripts, fonts or media. The company that hosts the site may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and requested page) for security and operation. We do not collect personal information through the site except what you choose to send us by email.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game or the website changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: {{email}}. For gameplay help or to report an inappropriate advertisement, see Support.
